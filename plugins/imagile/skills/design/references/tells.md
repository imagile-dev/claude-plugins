# Tells of generated design

Last reviewed 2026-10. These are the defaults that generated interfaces reach for whatever the subject is. Each one is legitimate for some brief. Each one is a default, not a choice.

## How to use this list

- **The brief wins.** If `DESIGN.md` or the user pins one of these looks, use it exactly. This list governs only the axes nobody has decided.
- **Replace a tell with a procedure, not a substitute.** Never answer "no Inter" with "use Satoshi". Every published list of approved alternatives became the next wave of tells. Run the font procedure in `typography.md` or the color strategy in `color.md` instead.
- **Removal is not enough.** A page with every tell stripped and nothing added reads as thin and timid. Fill the space with scale, real imagery, full content or a color field.
- **Watch the second-order reflex.** "Not the SaaS look" has its own default: an editorial italic serif, mono labels and hairline rules. Avoiding the first wave by reaching for the third is still a reflex.
- **Date this file.** Tells change every few months. Review it when the models change, and delete entries that no longer appear.

## The recurring looks

Generated pages cluster around these complete looks:

1. **Warm editorial.** A cream background near `#F4F1EA`, a high-contrast serif display, a terracotta or clay accent near `#D97757`.
2. **Dark acid.** A near-black background with one bright acid-green or vermilion accent.
3. **Broadsheet.** Hairline rules, zero radius, dense newspaper columns, mono metadata.
4. **SaaS card kit.** Content chopped into identical rounded cards, one radius on everything, the same soft gray shadow under each, gradient washes behind.
5. **AI glow.** Purple-to-blue gradients, cyan on dark, neon glows, glass panels, glowing orbs.

## Palette

- Violet, indigo and purple-to-blue gradients with no reason in the subject
- Gradient text
- Glows, radial spotlights and halos behind content
- Glassmorphism as decoration rather than a layer that needs to show depth
- Pure `#000` or `#fff`, or a tinted near-black like `#0B0B0B` standing in for black by habit
- A gradient wash behind every section

## Type

- One word of a headline accented in italic, bold or a different color
- A tracked-out uppercase eyebrow above every heading. Keep an eyebrow only when it carries information the heading does not, and at most one per three sections
- Monospace for small labels as a "technical" costume
- Meta strings joined with middle dots, such as `A · B · C`
- Labels built as `WORD — fragment` with a spaced dash
- An arrow appended to every link and button label
- A display size that is barely larger than the body

## Layout

- Everything centered
- The hero metric: one big number, a small label, three supporting stats
- A row of three identical cards with an icon tile, a heading and two lines
- Rounded icon tiles above every card heading
- Cards nested inside cards
- Numbered markers like `01 / 02 / 03` on content that is not a sequence
- A bento grid with empty cells or more cells than content
- Colored stripe borders on one side of a card
- The same layout repeated in every section
- A section huddled on one side with half the width empty
- A floating pill navigation bar on a page that does not need one

## Motion

- A fade-and-slide-up entrance on every section
- A hover lift on every card
- Bounce or elastic easing
- Pulsing "live" dots, blinking cursors, marquees with no content reason
- Scramble or typewriter text, custom cursors, scroll hijacking, preloaders

## Imagery

- Stock photos of handshakes, laptops and smiling teams
- Glowing brain or orb "AI" imagery, lens flare, glossy 3D blobs
- Colored placeholder blocks where a real image belongs
- Fake dashboards built from gray bars
- Emoji or unicode glyphs used as icons
- Illustrations assembled from basic SVG shapes
- Art in a dark rectangle on a page that is not dark

## Copy

- Elevate, seamless, unleash, empower, supercharge, streamline, world-class
- "Not just X, it's Y" and "No X. Just Y."
- Tricolons that exist for rhythm, not meaning
- "Get started" and "Learn more" as the only calls to action
- "John Doe", "Acme", "Nexus", `99.99%`, `1,234,567`
- Invented logos, testimonials and "trusted by 10,000+ teams"
- Habitual dashes in every sentence

## Content honesty

- Proof that does not exist
- Totals and deltas that do not add up from the parts shown
- Sample data with no marker that it is sample data
